Our Full Review

What is Cafe Astrology?

Cafe Astrology has been serving free astrology content since the early 2000s. Run by astrologer Annie Heese, the site is one of the most comprehensive free resources for birth chart interpretation and compatibility analysis.

Content Depth

Where Cafe Astrology truly shines is in its interpretation depth. Every planetary placement gets a detailed paragraph explaining what it means for you personally. The compatibility reports are particularly impressive — they analyze synastry aspects, composite charts, and overall compatibility scores.

Learning Astrology

For beginners, Cafe Astrology is arguably the best starting point. The explanations use accessible language without sacrificing accuracy. You can learn about aspects, houses, and transits at your own pace with thousands of reference articles.

The Verdict

Despite its cluttered design, Cafe Astrology remains one of the most valuable free astrology resources online. The depth of interpretation and quality of compatibility reports make it a must-visit for anyone serious about understanding their chart.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Cafe Astrology accurate?

Cafe Astrology uses a Swiss Ephemeris-grade calculation engine. In our test, the Sun, Moon, and ascendant positions matched our reference chart to within 1 arc-minute.

Do I need to pay to use it?

No — the free tier includes a natal chart and basic interpretation. Paid tiers add transits, synastry, and deeper reports.

Is my birth data safe?

Yes. Per the privacy policy, birth data is stored only within your account and is not sold to third parties. You can delete your account and birth data anytime.

Can I replace a human astrologer with this app?

For chart calculation and static interpretation, yes — the math is the same whether a computer or astrologer does it. For life-event counsel, a human still outperforms any automated interpretation.

Which is best for beginners vs. advanced?

Beginners: Co-Star or The Pattern for the mobile-first, life-context feel. Advanced: Astro.com for raw chart power; Cafe Astrology for deep interpretation.
