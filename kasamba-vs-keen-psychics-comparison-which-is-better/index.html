Kasamba vs Keen Psychics: Which Platform Is Worth It

Adelaide Brun · June 12, 2026 · 18 min read

If you have been searching for a thorough kasamba vs keen psychics comparison which is better analysis, you have landed in the right place. Both services dominate the English-language psychic marketplace and have loyal communities built around them, yet they are not interchangeable. Understanding where each one excels — and where each one falls short — can save you real money and real frustration.

Platform Backgrounds and Core Identities

Before diving into features, it helps to understand what each platform was built to be, because their founding philosophies shape everything from advisor quality to customer support culture.

Kasamba: A Specialist Chat-First Network

Kasamba launched in 1999 under the name LivePerson Experts and has operated under the Kasamba brand since the mid-2000s. Its interface was designed around text chat from the very beginning, which means the experience is tightly optimised for written exchanges rather than voice or video. The platform positions itself as a destination for spiritual counselling broadly defined — astrology, tarot, dream analysis, numerology, career advice, and relationship guidance all sit side by side in the same directory.

Kasamba enforces a minimum rate floor for advisors but does not publish a hard cap, so you can find readers charging anywhere from about $1.99 per minute all the way past $30 per minute for senior-tier practitioners. New users receive three free minutes with any advisor, which is one of the most recognisable introductory offers in the industry.

Keen: A Voice-and-Chat Hybrid Built Around Community

Keen was founded in 1999 as well, originally as Intelicast, and rebranded into its current form in the early 2000s. Keen has historically been stronger in phone readings than Kasamba, even though it now supports text chat and even video for many advisors. Its community features — including an advice forum, horoscope content, and a longer public review trail per advisor — give it a slightly different character. Keen tends to attract repeat customers who build ongoing relationships with a single advisor over months or years.

Keen’s pricing is broadly comparable to Kasamba’s, with most working advisors charging between $1.99 and $9.99 per minute, though high-profile readers can go above $20. New users get ten minutes for $1.99 as the standard introductory offer, which provides more clock time than Kasamba’s three free minutes — an important distinction we will return to.

Pricing Structures Compared

Cost is typically the first thing people want to understand, and the two platforms handle it in ways that look similar on the surface but diverge in practical terms.

Kasamba Pricing Breakdown

  • Introductory offer: Three free minutes with every new advisor you try (resettable per advisor, not a one-time offer)
  • Standard rates: Most active advisors sit between $1.99 and $12.99 per minute
  • Premium advisors: Top-rated readers can charge $20–$35+ per minute
  • Billing: Per-minute, charged at the end of the session or when your prepaid balance runs down
  • Prepaid packages: Kasamba offers discounted credit bundles (e.g., buying $50 or $100 at once often unlocks a bonus percentage)
  • Refund policy: Kasamba offers a satisfaction guarantee — if you are unhappy with a reading, you can contact support and typically receive credit back, though full cash refunds are rare and discretionary

One practical note: the three free minutes reset when you try a different advisor, not when you start a new session with the same one. This means comparison-shopping is relatively low-risk on Kasamba — you can sample three or four advisors for free before committing real money.

Keen Pricing Breakdown

  • Introductory offer: Ten minutes for $1.99 on your first session with any advisor (one-time per account, not per advisor)
  • Standard rates: Most working advisors charge $1.99–$9.99 per minute
  • Premium advisors: Top names charge $15–$25+ per minute
  • Billing: Per-minute; Keen also allows you to set a spending limit before a call begins, which is a genuinely useful feature for budget-conscious users
  • Prepaid balance: You can top up your account in set increments
  • Refund policy: Keen has a formal satisfaction guarantee for first-time readings; repeat customers often find goodwill credits easier to obtain than at some rivals

The ten-minutes-for-$1.99 deal gives Keen a stronger numerical first impression, but it is one-time-only. Once you exhaust that introduction, you are paying full rate like everyone else. Kasamba’s per-advisor free minutes give more flexibility to explore the roster over time, especially if you are still working out which specialty or communication style suits you.

Which Platform Is Cheaper Long-Term?

On balance, neither platform is dramatically cheaper than the other at standard rates. If you plan to use psychic services regularly, the rate per minute charged by your preferred advisor matters far more than the introductory deal structure. Both platforms also run periodic promotional discounts, so checking current offers on Astrology Service Reviews before you commit is a smart habit.

Advisor Quality and Vetting

Pricing means little if the advisors are unreliable. This is arguably the most important dimension in any kasamba vs keen psychics comparison which is better evaluation.

How Kasamba Screens Its Advisors

Kasamba has stated publicly that it reviews applications before approving new advisors, though the specific criteria it applies are not published in detail. Advisors must demonstrate competence in their declared specialties through an internal review process. The platform removes advisors who receive consistent negative feedback, and it maintains a public rating system with written reviews from paying customers.

In practice, the quality distribution on Kasamba is broad. Because the platform has a relatively large roster (several hundred active advisors at any given time), you will find a wide range from impressive practitioners who have cultivated loyal followings to newer or lower-rated advisors whose skills are less proven. The key safeguards for users are the rating/review system and the free-minutes offer, which together allow you to screen advisors before spending significantly.

How Keen Screens Its Advisors

Keen’s vetting process is similarly described in general terms — applicants are reviewed and tested — but Keen leans more heavily on its community review infrastructure as an ongoing quality filter. Keen’s advisor profiles include a longer public history of star ratings and written testimonials, often stretching back several years, which makes it easier to spot advisors with sustained track records versus those who are newer or inconsistent.

Keen also publishes response rates and average response times for advisors who accept messages, which helps users gauge availability before choosing someone.

Reading Format Differences

This is an underappreciated distinction in the comparison:

  • Kasamba is almost exclusively chat-based. If you prefer the anonymity and pace of text conversation, or if you find voice readings anxiety-inducing, Kasamba is the more natural home.
  • Keen supports phone, chat, and video depending on the advisor. If you want to hear a voice — or if you process information better through spoken conversation — Keen’s phone option is a genuine differentiator.

Neither format is inherently more accurate. Many practitioners are equally effective over text or voice. But personal preference matters enormously to how comfortable and open you feel during a session, which in turn affects how useful the reading tends to be.

Specialties and Advisor Categories

Both platforms cover the standard range of metaphysical services, but with slightly different emphasis.

What Kasamba Does Well

Kasamba’s strongest categories, based on advisor volume and user review depth, tend to be:

  • Love and relationships — the single most-searched category on most psychic platforms
  • Tarot — a large pool of practitioners across multiple traditions
  • Dream analysis — a specialty where Kasamba has disproportionately many experienced readers
  • Astrology — natal charts, synastry, transits, and predictive work

For detailed written exchanges about emotional situations — processing a breakup, evaluating a career decision, understanding a family dynamic — Kasamba’s chat-first format often works particularly well.

What Keen Does Well

Keen’s strongest areas include:

  • Astrology — a notably large astrology section with advisors who specialize in specific branches (Vedic, Western, evolutionary)
  • Spiritual life coaching — advisors who blend metaphysical insight with practical guidance
  • Medium and channeling sessions — a category where Keen has built a recognisable cohort of long-tenured practitioners
  • Numerology and life path guidance

Keen also has a more developed content ecosystem surrounding its readings — horoscopes, articles, and community posts — which some users find valuable as supplementary material.

User Experience and Platform Features

A psychic reading platform is only as good as the experience of actually using it, and there are meaningful UX differences between these two services.

Kasamba’s Interface and Tools

  • Clean, category-browsable directory with filters for specialty, price, and availability
  • Real-time chat window that preserves the full transcript after the session
  • Ability to save favourite advisors and receive notifications when they come online
  • Mobile app available for iOS and Android with full feature parity
  • Session transcripts can be accessed and re-read, which is useful for reflection after the fact

One known frustration: Kasamba’s search and filter tools are functional but not particularly sophisticated. If you want to narrow by specific sub-specialties (e.g., specifically Vedic astrology rather than astrology generally), you may need to browse manually rather than filter precisely.

Keen’s Interface and Tools

  • More granular advisor directory with specific skill tags
  • Pre-session messaging feature — you can send a short message to an advisor before committing to a paid session to confirm availability or describe your situation
  • Spending cap feature (set your maximum before the call begins — genuinely useful)
  • Detailed advisor profiles with reading statistics, history, and rate history
  • Mobile app for iOS and Android
  • Community forum and editorial content accessible within the platform

Keen’s pre-session messaging is one of its standout practical features. Being able to describe what you are looking for and get a brief response before paying helps confirm whether an advisor is a good fit, reducing the chance of wasting money on a session that goes nowhere.

Red Flags to Watch on Either Platform

No psychic platform is free of bad actors, and being an informed consumer matters. At Astrology Service Reviews, we track the patterns that separate legitimate practitioners from those worth avoiding.

Universal Red Flags

  • Promises of guaranteed outcomes — no ethical practitioner promises to bring back an ex, remove a curse for a fee, or guarantee a specific future event
  • Pressure to buy more time mid-session — a sign the advisor is padding the session rather than delivering content
  • Requests for additional payments outside the platform — always a scam signal; legitimate advisors keep all transactions on-platform
  • Extremely low review counts with uniformly perfect scores — may indicate fake or self-generated reviews
  • Vague openings with no specific detail — cold-reading tactics that never move to genuine insight

Platform-Specific Cautions

On Kasamba:
– Be aware that the three free minutes are short. An experienced practitioner can provide meaningful content in three minutes, but some advisors use that window for slow introductions. If an advisor spends the free minutes asking rather than delivering, treat it as data.
– Check the date of the most recent reviews, not just the star average. An advisor with a 4.8 rating but no new reviews in 18 months may no longer be active or engaged.

On Keen:
– Keen’s phone format means session costs can escalate faster than expected if you are not monitoring time. Use the spending cap feature — it exists precisely for this reason.
– Because Keen advisors can appear offline for extended periods, you may find some listed advisors are very rarely available. Sort by recent activity rather than rating alone.

Practical Tips for Getting the Most Value

Before Your First Session

  1. Decide on your communication preference — if voice matters to you, start with Keen; if you prefer text or anonymity, start with Kasamba.
  2. Write down your questions — vague sessions produce vague readings; specific questions lead to specific, actionable insights.
  3. Set a realistic budget — decide your maximum spend before you open the app, and use Keen’s spending cap or Kasamba’s prepaid balance to enforce it.
  4. Read at least ten reviews per advisor — focus on reviews that describe the specific type of reading you want (love, career, grief, etc.).
  5. Sample multiple advisors — on Kasamba, use the per-advisor free minutes strategically. On Keen, your $1.99 intro is a one-shot, so choose the advisor for it carefully.

During the Session

  • Provide relevant context but do not lead the reader; see what they pick up on independently before filling in details.
  • Note the time — especially on phone calls, where it is easy to lose track.
  • Do not share financial information beyond what the platform itself handles for payment.

After the Session

  • Review your transcript (Kasamba) or take notes immediately after a phone call (Keen).
  • Rate honestly — both platforms rely on community reviews to surface quality, and your rating helps future users.
  • Give yourself a few days before booking a follow-up; processing time allows you to evaluate whether the reading resonated rather than making decisions in the emotional moment.

The Bottom Line: Which Platform Should You Choose?

The kasamba vs keen psychics comparison which is better question does not have a single universally correct answer — it depends on your priorities.

Choose Kasamba if:
– You strongly prefer text-based communication
– You want to sample multiple advisors with minimal upfront commitment (per-advisor free minutes)
– Dream analysis, tarot, or detailed love-and-relationship readings are your primary interest
– You prefer to read transcripts after sessions for reflection

Choose Keen if:
– You want the option of a phone or video reading
– You value a richer pre-session research experience (more detailed profiles, pre-session messaging, longer review histories)
– You want a spending cap as a built-in budget control
– You are looking for astrology specialists or spiritual life coaching

Both platforms are legitimate, both have been operating for over two decades, and both have produced meaningful experiences for many people. Neither is a scam — though both contain individual advisors worth approaching with healthy scepticism, as any large marketplace does.

If you are still undecided, the practical move is to try both introductory offers: Kasamba’s per-advisor free minutes let you sample the chat-first experience at no cost, and Keen’s $1.99 for ten minutes gives you a substantive first phone or chat session with a carefully chosen advisor. Your own experience after those two trials will tell you more than any written comparison can.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Kasamba or Keen better for first-time users?

Kasamba is slightly more beginner-friendly because its per-advisor free minutes let you sample several readers at no cost before committing. Keen’s $1.99 introductory deal offers more time but is a one-time offer, so it rewards users who have already done enough research to pick a strong first advisor.

Which platform has cheaper per-minute rates?

Both platforms are broadly comparable in standard rates, with most active advisors charging between $1.99 and $9.99 per minute on each service. Keen’s advisor pool tends to cluster slightly lower on average, but the difference is not dramatic. The specific advisor you choose matters far more than the platform average.

Can I get a refund if I am unhappy with a reading on either platform?

Both Kasamba and Keen offer satisfaction guarantees, though the specifics vary. Kasamba offers credit-based refunds for unsatisfactory readings on a case-by-case basis. Keen has a more formalised first-reading satisfaction guarantee and is generally considered accessible when it comes to goodwill credits for repeat customers. Cash refunds are rarely offered by either platform — credits back to your account balance are the standard resolution.

Does Keen offer phone readings, or is it chat only like Kasamba?

Keen supports phone, chat, and video readings depending on the individual advisor’s settings. This makes it more versatile for users who prefer voice communication. Kasamba is primarily text-chat based, though a small number of advisors on the platform do offer phone options. If voice readings are important to you, Keen is the stronger choice.

Are there red flags I should watch for on both platforms?

Yes. On either platform, avoid advisors who make specific guarantees (bringing back an ex, removing curses for escalating fees, predicting exact future events), pressure you to extend sessions beyond your comfort, or request payments outside the platform’s official system. On Kasamba, watch for advisors who use the free minutes purely for slow introductions rather than substantive engagement. On Keen, always use the spending cap feature on phone calls to prevent unexpectedly large bills.
